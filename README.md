\# Flask E-Commerce Management System



A full-stack e-commerce management system developed using Python, Flask, MySQL, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap.



\## Features



\### Admin Panel

\- Admin Login / Authentication

\- Dashboard

\- Product Management

\- Category Management

\- Brand Management

\- Customer Management

\- Supplier Management

\- Sales Management

\- Purchase Management

\- Expense Management

\- Stock History

\- Order Management

\- Sales Returns

\- Purchase Returns

\- Coupon Management

\- Reviews Management

\- Wishlist Management

\- Reports

\- Product Search and Sorting

\- Product Image Upload

\- PDF Invoices



\### Customer Side

\- Product Browsing

\- Product Details

\- Shopping Cart

\- Checkout

\- Order Placement

\- Wishlist

\- Product Reviews

\- Customer Account
